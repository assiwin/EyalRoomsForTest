10.0.0

New in this version:
- Added the optional "בודד בשולחן" mock-exam arrangement.
- In single-desk mode, ordinary rooms contain at most 20 students.
- The preferred limit is 10 students from each unit category, with a controlled relaxation up to 13 when needed to reduce rooms or teacher splits.
- Units 3 and 3א are counted as one category in single-desk mode, and more than two categories may share a room.
- Existing reduced, separate, and class-7 room logic remains unchanged.
- Exam-grade selection now appears inline with the main application title.
- The exam-time calculator occupies 50% of the available desktop width and remains full width on small screens.
- Added a "צור PDF" button to the right of Reset. The generated A4 schedule PDF is saved in Output and the folder opens automatically.
- Installer upgraded to Version 10 while retaining previous-install cleanup and preserving Output.
